FlowerBox — a Soulslike prototype
=================================

Run
---
Windows: double-click flowerbox.exe
Linux:   ./flowerbox

Keep the "assets" folder next to the program (fonts and level files live there).
Settings and your save go to your user folder (Windows: %APPDATA%\flowerbox,
Linux: ~/.config/flowerbox and ~/.local/share/flowerbox). Start with --new-game for a fresh save.

Controls (keyboard + mouse; rebind them in Esc -> Controls)
-----------------------------------------------------------
W A S D        move              Mouse        camera
Space          jump              Left Shift   tap: roll, hold: sprint
Left mouse     right hand: tap light, hold heavy attack
Right mouse    left hand: block / parry (shield) or attack (weapon)
Mouse wheel    hold + a mouse button: two-hand that weapon; tap: lock on
Q              lock on (flick the mouse to switch target)
F              weapon ability     R           drink Healing Ale
E              interact / rest at the sunflower
Tab / I        inventory          T           chat
O              online (host / join)
Esc            menu (settings: name, skin, sensitivity, volume, ...)
A gamepad works too (Souls layout).

Combat notes
------------
Behind a small humanoid enemy, a light attack is a backstab. Parry an attack, or
break an enemy's stance (the thin gold bar fills as you hit it), then a light
attack is a riposte. Kills give Light (top right), shared by everyone online.
Ladders: E at the top or the foot, W / S to climb, hold Shift going down to slide.

Playing together
----------------
One player presses O and H (host): the join code is copied to the clipboard.
Send it to your friends; they copy it, press O and J (join). Up to 5 players, no server needed.
Everybody needs the same version of the game.
Fall in a boss fight while others still fight: you watch them (Q / E switches
who) until it ends. If everyone falls, the world resets as after a rest.
